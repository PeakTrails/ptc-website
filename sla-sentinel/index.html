How It Works

Six steps. From contract clause
to negotiating advantage.

SLA Sentinel begins with a structured mapping engagement — your contracts team, your network team, and Gemini working together to turn contract language into measurable thresholds. Every step after that runs automatically, building a verified performance record that compounds in value over time.

Step 01
🗺️

Map

Your contracts team and network team work with us — with Gemini's help — to translate SLA penalty clauses into measurable thresholds. We identify what your existing monitoring platform already collects and what may need to be configured. The output is a detection ruleset your team owns.

Step 02
📡

Detect

Your existing monitoring platform identifies a service condition — uptime below SLA commitment, response time breach, or error rate trigger. No new monitoring infrastructure required.

Step 03
⚡

Ingest

A structured event is sent to Google Cloud, validated, normalized with vendor identity, and written to a permanent breach record.

Step 04
🧠

Analyze

With Gemini's help, the relevant vendor contract is read, the applicable penalty clause is identified, and financial exposure is calculated — including penalty tiers, measurement windows, and exclusion conditions.

Step 05
📋

Notify

Leadership receives a structured breach summary: vendor name, outage duration, SLA threshold breached, financial penalty calculated, and recommended next action.

Step 06
🗂️

Record

Every breach event, calculation, and leadership action is permanently logged — available for vendor dispute resolution, audit, compliance reporting, and contract renewal negotiations.
