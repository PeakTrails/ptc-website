A proven path from foundation to full capability. Every stage builds on what came before — the investments you make early compound at every stage that follows.

The Complete Path

Ten stages from first step to full capability

Most organizations start at Stage 1 and go as far as their goals require. Each stage is achievable — nothing requires a data science team or a complete platform replacement.

Each Stage Explained

What happens at each stage —
and what you get

Every stage is designed to deliver value on its own. You do not need to commit to the full journey to start — begin where you are and build from there.

Stage 01

Landing Zone

We define the complete scope of your Google Cloud foundation — security requirements, governance policies, network architecture, and deployment standards. Your implementation partner builds from a fully specified blueprint.

Stage 02

Use Case Roadmap

We work with your teams to identify and prioritize the AI opportunities that will deliver real value. You get a clear, sequenced roadmap tied to your actual business goals — not a generic list of AI possibilities.

Stage 03

Data Preparation

We assess and prepare your data so Gemini can actually use it — clean, accessible, and connected to the right systems. Your data is ready to power AI rather than sitting in silos that no agent can reach.

Stage 04

MCP Integration

We scope the connections between Gemini and your existing enterprise systems — your ticketing platform, your facilities system, your HR tools. You get a blueprint for how your platforms become AI-accessible without rebuilding anything.

Stage 05

NotebookLM

We use Google's NotebookLM to test how Gemini interacts with your actual data — low risk, no code, immediate insight. You see what Gemini can and cannot do with your information before committing to a build.

Stage 06

Gemini Agents

We configure purpose-built agents directly in the Gemini Enterprise interface — no custom development required. You get working AI agents your staff can use immediately, built on the platform you already own.

Stage 07

Agentic Workflows

We design and configure multi-step workflows where agents hand off tasks to each other automatically. You get orchestrated AI processes that handle complex, multi-stage work without human intervention at every step.

Stage 08

Agent Ecosystem

We connect your agents to each other and to Google's Agent Marketplace — accessing pre-built solutions alongside your custom ones. Your agents collaborate rather than operate in isolation, and you gain access to a growing catalog of ready-to-deploy solutions.

Stage 09

Purpose-Built Agentic Solutions

We scope and prototype the complex solutions your organization needs, then connect you with the right development partner to build and maintain them at scale. You get a fully defined solution and a qualified partner to deliver it.

Feedback, Reporting and Continuous Improvement

This stage does not come last — it runs underneath every other stage from the moment you start. We establish the measurement framework that collects signal across your entire AI footprint, tracks what is working, and feeds improvements back into the roadmap.
