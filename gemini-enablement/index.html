How It Works

Six phases. Six weeks.

Each phase has a defined scope, a defined ask of your team, and a defined set of deliverables. No surprises.

1

Acceptance — Environment Readiness

What Happens

GCP foundations build is reviewed and confirmed ready. Any issues blocking rollout are identified and resolved before anyone touches the platform.

Your Team

IT lead provides access and attends a kickoff call. Estimated time: 1 hour.

You Receive

  • Written environment readiness confirmation
  • Gap resolution before user provisioning
  • Kickoff notes and weekly status cadence
2

Technical Pilot + IT Training

What Happens

Your 3 IT admins are first on the platform. We validate SSO, security, and audit logging before any business users are provisioned — then deliver their 90-minute training session.

Your Team

3 IT admins participate in pilot and attend training. ~3 hours total time commitment.

You Receive

  • All 3 IT admins active on platform
  • IT configuration training delivered
  • Written GCP admin how-to guide
  • SSO, security, audit logging validated
3

User Intake

What Happens

We meet with each of your 3 departments in a focused 30–45 minute conversation to learn what staff do every day and identify immediate use cases. Workshops are then built around your workflows.

Your Team

Department representatives attend one 30–45 minute intake conversation. No preparation required.

You Receive

  • Use case alignment document per department
  • 3 customized workshop decks for your workflows
  • All 5 sessions scheduled with calendar invites
4

Training

What Happens

All 15 licensed users attend sessions tailored to their role. Business users attend the shared basics session and their department workshop. IT admins completed their session in Phase 2.

Your Team

Business users: 2 sessions (60-min + 90-min). IT admins: already complete from Phase 2.

You Receive

  • 5 sessions delivered — all remote
  • Session decks and exercise guides to keep
  • Post-session follow-up notes
  • Attendee feedback summary
5

Hands-On Configuration

What Happens

One AI tool is configured for each of your 3 departments — grounded in their actual documents, built around their specific use cases. IT admins shadow every step.

Your Team

Dept reps: 30–60 minutes per configuration session. IT admins shadow all configuration work.

You Receive

  • 3 live configured AI tools — one per department
  • Each tool tested against real use cases
  • IT capable of independent replication
  • Office hours for adoption support
6

IT Handoff

What Happens

Platform ownership formally transfers to your IT team with full documentation. IT was trained and hands-on throughout — this phase is a confirmation, not a last-minute crash course.

Your Team

IT lead attends a 30-minute handoff call to confirm ownership transfer. No additional time required.

You Receive

  • IT admin guide — user mgmt, audit logs, troubleshooting
  • Self-sufficiency checklist
  • Prioritized next steps document
  • Full platform ownership transferred

FAQ

Common questions.

Do we need a GCP foundations build before this engagement?+
Yes. This engagement assumes your GCP environment is in place and Gemini Enterprise licenses are active. If you need a foundations build first, we can do that for you as well.
Why 3 departments and 4 users each?+
The 15-user structure is designed to give each department enough coverage for meaningful adoption while keeping the engagement focused. It is not a full organizational rollout — it is a structured pilot that gives you proven tools and capable IT staff to expand from.
What if our department structure is different?+
The 3 × 4 structure is a starting point. Contact us to discuss how the engagement can be adapted to your organization.
Do we need technical staff to participate?+
Yes — your IT lead needs to be available for the technical pilot, configuration sessions, and handoff. Business users only need to attend their intake conversation and two sessions.
Who owns the training materials?+
Session decks and exercise guides are provided to your organization for internal use. The underlying curriculum is retained by Peak Trails Consulting.
What happens after the engagement ends?+
Your IT team owns the platform. The next steps document provides a prioritized roadmap for what to build next. Peak Trails is available for follow-on engagements if you want to expand into additional departments or more advanced configurations.
Can this be procured through a cooperative purchasing vehicle?+
