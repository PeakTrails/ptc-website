How It Works

From introduction
to activation

The partner structure is designed to make engaging easy for SLED clients — established relationships, streamlined procurement, and senior technical expertise from day one.

1

Google or NTS Introduction

A Google Public Sector account rep or New Tech Solutions identifies an organization that has purchased Gemini Enterprise and needs activation support. Peak Trails is brought in as the technical enablement partner.

2

Discovery and Scoping

Peak Trails conducts the AI Discovery engagement — assessing the organization's readiness, identifying use cases, and delivering a prioritized roadmap. NTS handles the commercial and procurement aspects.

3

Activation and Enablement

Peak Trails activates the Gemini environment, trains staff, configures the first agents, and establishes governance. The client moves from a purchased license to a working AI capability.
