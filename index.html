Governance

What does AI Governance actually
mean for your organization?

Governance is one of the most overused words in technology — and one of the least explained. In practical terms it means three things.

01

Who can use it and how

Which staff have access to Gemini, what data they can interact with, and what they are permitted to do with the outputs. Clear policies prevent both under-use and misuse.

02

What goes in and what stays private

Your organization has sensitive data — student records, personnel files, constituent information. Governance defines what Gemini can see, what it cannot, and how those boundaries are enforced.

03

How you stay in control as it evolves

Google updates Gemini Enterprise regularly. Governance gives you a framework for evaluating what to adopt, what to wait on, and how to communicate changes to your teams without creating anxiety.
